VERSION B · WIX STUDIO ADAPTATIONS (hide via Tweaks)

  • Header: solid navy instead of translucent blur.
  • Form in two steps, built as two separate Wix Forms (not one multi-step form), so the lead is saved after step 1 even if the visitor stops there. Form 1 (name, email, radio buttons) redirects to /catholic-curious/thanks. GA4 counts that page view as the conversion. That page holds Form 2 (phone and comments, optional) plus an "I'm all set" button.
  • Thank-you messages don't include the visitor's name. A form-start event isn't tracked without GTM.
  • FAQ: native Accordion element.
  • Info night: date is written as "Third Thursday of every month". Add to calendar comes from a Wix Events listing.
  • Videos: native Wix Video Player instead of click-to-load thumbnails.
  • Mobile CTA bar: "Pin to screen" on mobile. It's always visible; it can't hide while the form is on screen.
  • Hover: color and border changes only. No lift animation.
  • "What is Catholic Curious" video: native Wix Video Box, cropped to 9:16. Settings: autoplay, muted, loop, dark overlay and a play button. Wix can't restart the video from the beginning with sound on click without Velo; the closest native option is "Play on click" with controls, or a second Video Box in a lightbox that plays the full video with sound.
  • Stories: the three testimonials are illustrative examples. Replace them with real ones, with permission, before publishing.
  • Info night card: Wix Studio supports a native "Backdrop filter" (blur) on containers. Use blur 18px plus a semi-transparent white fill for the glass effect.
  • Quote: in Wix, use a native entrance animation (Fade In or Reveal, slow, once) on the quote, line and attribution, staggered. The scripted blur reveal is only in the mockup.
  • Seasonal ribbon: a strip section you show or hide in the editor.
  • Tracking: give every CTA the ID shown in the tracking overlay. Track tel: and mailto: clicks with GTM click triggers via Marketing Integrations.

Three simple steps.
